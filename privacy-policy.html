When you use our lead generation form, we collect minimal and necessary personal details required to assist you with loan processing:

The information collected is utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate your loan application or credit score check through our authorized lending partners (OneAndro / NBFCs / Banks).

3. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is securely processed and shared exclusively with verified banking and lending partners for loan processing under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Consent

By using our website and submitting your details, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to allow Quick Loan Desk to contact you regarding financial services.
